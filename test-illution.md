PHASE 1: DIRECTIONAL ALIGNMENT
Is CTG moving with the herd or against it?
═══════════════════════════════════════════════

Evaluate whether the bank's current strategic positioning, 
balance sheet structure, and business model are aligned with — 
or working against — the eight structural forces above.

Alignment is not a matter of intent or management narrative. 
It is a matter of mechanics. A bank is aligned if its actual 
capital flows, collateral composition, funding structure, and 
regulatory position allow it to operate within — and benefit 
from — the state-directed architecture. A bank is misaligned 
if its mechanics require it to fight the current to survive.

Produce a directional verdict on each of the following vectors:

  VECTOR 1 — FUNDING STRUCTURE
  Is the bank's liability side positioned to survive the 
  deposit war? What is its CASA ratio, its reliance on 
  interbank funding, and its exposure to the 110% LDR ceiling?

  VECTOR 2 — CAPITAL ADEQUACY
  Where does the bank sit relative to the Basel III thresholds 
  of Circular 14? Is it above the CAR ceiling with room to 
  expand, at the minimum and paralyzed, or below and existentially 
  threatened? Is it restricted from paying dividends?

  VECTOR 3 — ASSET COMPOSITION
  What percentage of the bank's loan book is concentrated in 
  commercial real estate versus state-directed priority sectors 
  (agriculture, social housing, SMEs, infrastructure)? 
  Under Circular 14 risk weights, does its current asset mix 
  consume or preserve capital?

  VECTOR 4 — COLLATERAL QUALITY
  What proportion of the bank's collateral base is real estate? 
  Of that, how much is subject to the administrative paralysis 
  freeze — pending zoning approvals, incomplete title transfers, 
  or unresolved legal status? What is the bank's real, 
  provisioning-adjusted NPL exposure?

  VECTOR 5 — INFRASTRUCTURE MANDATE EXPOSURE
  Is the bank a designated participant in the VND 500 trillion 
  preferential credit program? If so, what is its allocated 
  share, and what is the implied negative carry against its 
  current cost of funds?

  VECTOR 6 — BOND MARKET EXPOSURE
  What is the bank's total holding of corporate bonds? 
  What proportion is real estate sector paper? 
  Is the bank a net issuer or net buyer in the current market? 
  What is its exposure to the 2026 maturity cliff?

Final Directional Output — choose one:

  ▶ MOVING WITH THE CURRENT
    The bank's mechanics are structurally compatible with the 
    state-directed financial architecture. It is positioned to 
    capture flows as weaker institutions are forced out.

  ▶ MOVING ACROSS THE CURRENT
    The bank's mechanics are partially compatible. It benefits 
    from some structural forces but is exposed to others. 
    Survival is probable but growth is constrained.

  ▶ MOVING AGAINST THE CURRENT
    The bank's mechanics are structurally incompatible with the 
    current architecture. It is absorbing compounding pressure 
    across multiple vectors simultaneously and faces existential 
    stress without external intervention.

PHASE 2: INDIVIDUAL HEALTH (Only if Phase 1 
verdict is WITH or ACROSS the current)
Is this a strong cow or a weak cow?

If the bank is directionally aligned, evaluate its individual

  Is the bank's profitability driven by genuine net interest

  DIAGNOSTIC 2 — CAPITAL GENERATION CAPACITY
  Is the bank generating sufficient retained earnings to 
  organically build the capital buffers required by Circular 14

  What is the trajectory of its CET1 ratio?

  DIAGNOSTIC 3 — FRANCHISE STRENGTH
  Does the bank possess structural competitive advantages that 
  are durable in the current environment — specifically a high 
  and stable CASA base, a dominant position in state-directed 
  lending channels, or a technology infrastructure that reduces 
  cost of acquisition? Or does it compete purely on deposit 
  rate, which is a race to the bottom?

  DIAGNOSTIC 4 — MANAGEMENT OF THE TRILEMMA
  How is the bank's management navigating the irresolvable 
  policy trilemma? Is there evidence of deliberate balance 
  sheet positioning — reducing real estate exposure ahead of 
  regulatory tightening, pre-building capital buffers, 
  diversifying funding sources? Or is it reactive, waiting 
  for regulatory signals before acting?

  DIAGNOSTIC 5 — STRESS SCENARIO SURVIVAL
  Under the three identified break scenarios — Exchange Rate 
  Node, Asset Quality Node, Sovereign Fiscal Node — which 
  scenarios can this bank survive without state intervention? 
  Which scenarios require a bailout?

Final Individual Output — choose one:

  ▶ STRONG AND LEADING
    The bank is directionally aligned AND individually healthy. 
    Its mechanics allow it to capture market share as the herd 
    consolidates. It generates durable earnings, builds capital 
    organically, and can survive at least two of the three

  ▶ ALIGNED BUT FRAGILE

    It moves with the herd but lacks the condition to sustain

    external support — capital injection, forbearance extension, 
    or merger — in an adverse one.

  ▶ SURVIVING ON FORBEARANCE
    The bank appears aligned but its health metrics are 
    artificially supported by regulatory forbearance, evergreened 
    debt, or cosmetic capital raises. Its true condition is 
    masked. When the forbearance veil lifts, its actual position 
    will be revealed as closer to AGAINST THE CURRENT.
