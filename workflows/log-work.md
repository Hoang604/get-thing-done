<philosophy>
Silent, continuous daily ledger. Collect raw facts of what we accomplished, so user has raw material to easily draft daily reports. Focus on outcomes/facts and blockers.
</philosophy>

# Daily Log Update Workflow

1. Date: `YY-MM-DD`. Time: `HH:MM`.
2. Target file: `.gtd/daily-log/YY-MM-DD.md`.
3. Extract core facts from conversation context:
   - **Task/Context**: General objective.
   - **Action Taken/Impact**: What done, built, fixed.
   - **Blockers/Notes**: Hurdle or context.
4. Append facts as concise bullets under `## [HH:MM] - [Task Name]` heading at the bottom of `.gtd/daily-log/YY-MM-DD.md`.
